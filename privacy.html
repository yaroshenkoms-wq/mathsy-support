Privacy Policy

Mathsy does not collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not send anything to a server.

  • Your progress, settings and parent-report data are stored only on your device and are deleted together with the app.
  • Camera (optional): used only to photograph a math problem. The photo is recognized on the device and is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications (optional): daily practice reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
  • Text is read aloud by the built-in iOS speech synthesizer on the device.
  • Links to official exam websites open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.
